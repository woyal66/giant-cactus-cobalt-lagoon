Anchor Mate privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Location

Anchor Mate reads your phone’s location to show the boat on the chart, follow a track, and warn you if the boat leaves the anchor swing. Your position stays on your phone. It is not sold, and it is not sent to an Anchor Mate account (there are no accounts).

While an anchor watch or a recorded track is running, the app keeps reading your location with the screen locked, so the alarm can sound. A notification shows while this is happening. It stops when you lift the anchor or end the track.

Weather, tides and maps

To show forecasts and maps, the app asks these services for the area you are looking at or planning a passage to: Open-Meteo (wind, waves, currents, tides), the Bureau of Meteorology (tides), the satellite photo providers named in the chart menu, OpenFreeMap (the Chart map), AWS Terrain Tiles and this app’s own website (seabed depth and tide tables), NOAA (official US charts, if you turn them on) and, only if you turn them on, RainViewer (rain radar) and NASA GIBS (satellite cloud pictures). These requests include the map area and your phone’s internet address, as any web request does. They do not identify you and are not used to build a profile.

Remote watch (optional)

If you turn on Remote watch, anchor alerts with the boat’s position are sent through the ntfy notification service to a private channel that only your paired phones know. The service logs the channel name and internet address to limit abuse. Remote watch is off unless you set it up, and turning it off stops all sending.

Shared tracks (optional)

If you choose to share a track to the online track library, or share an anchorage (its position, depth, bottom, shelter and comment), it is published for other users to find. For a track that is its points, name, location label and comment. Your name and contact details are not sent. Shared tracks are stored with Supabase. Only the phone that shared a track can remove it, from Navigation, My tracks. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Your tracks and settings

Tracks, routes, boat settings and saved offline areas are stored on your phone. Backups are files you choose to save yourself, for example to Google Drive. You can delete everything by uninstalling the app.

Payments and ads

The app is free. It has no purchases, no ads and no analytics or tracking, so no payment or advertising data is collected.

Children

Anchor Mate is a boating tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Safety

Anchor Mate is a lookout aid, not an official chart. The skipper remains responsible for navigation and the safety of the vessel.
